HOW TO REACH BVRIT, NARSAPUR

✈️

By Flight

Fastest way to reach the conference venue

The nearest airport is Rajiv Gandhi International Airport located at Shamshabad, Hyderabad.

🛬 Airport

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport
Hyderabad – Telangana

1

Book an Outstation Cab to BVRIT or travel by Pushpak Bus to Jubilee Bus Station.

2

Board TSRTC buses towards Narsapur / Medak from Jubilee Bus Station.

⚠️ Travel Advice

Participants arriving after 5 PM are advised to stay overnight in Hyderabad and travel to the venue the following morning.

🚌

By Road

Comfortable bus and road connectivity

BVRIT is connected by TSRTC buses from Hyderabad, Sangareddy and Medak. Dedicated College Buses are also available for conference delegates.

🚏 Main Boarding Point

Jubilee Bus Station (JBS) → Narsapur → BVRIT Campus

1

Use scheduled BVRIT College Bus Services from Hyderabad.

2

Refer to the official transport schedule for pickup locations and timings.

⚠️ Travel Advice

Delegates arriving late should stay overnight in Hyderabad before travelling to the venue.

📄 View Transport Routes
🚆

By Train

Travel comfortably to the conference venue by rail

Hyderabad is well connected by rail from all major cities. Visitors can reach BVRIT through Secunderabad, Nampally, or Kacheguda Railway Stations.

🚉 Major Railway Stations

Secunderabad • Nampally • Kacheguda

⚠️ Travel Tip:
If you arrive after 5 PM, stay overnight in Hyderabad and travel to BVRIT the following morning.

Step From To Travel Option
1 Secunderabad Railway Station Gandimaisamma Bus 272 or Cab
Nampally Railway Station Gandimaisamma Bus 9X / 230 or Cab
Kacheguda Railway Station Gandimaisamma Bus 83J/272 or Cab
2 Gandimaisamma Narsapur District Service Bus
3 Narsapur Bus Stand BVRIT Campus Shared Auto Rickshaw
