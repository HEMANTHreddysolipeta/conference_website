About the Institute & Conference

B V Raju Institute of Technology

Established in 1997 under Sri Vishnu Educational Society, BVRIT is one of Telangana's leading autonomous engineering institutions.

B V Raju Institute of Technology (BVRIT) was established in 1997 under the auspices of the Sri Vishnu Educational Society (SVES) by the eminent philanthropist Padmabhushan Dr. B. V. Raju. Since 2014, the institute has been granted autonomous status by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is approved by AICTE and affiliated to JNTU Hyderabad, Telangana. The institute is guided by a governing body comprising distinguished professionals from IITs, premier engineering institutions, and government sectors, under the leadership of Sri K. V. Vishnu Raju. The institution follows a strong academic governance framework focused on continuous improvement, quality enhancement, and excellence in technical education.

The institute offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. The undergraduate programmes include Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning), Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science), Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Pharmaceutical Engineering, and Chemical Engineering. At the postgraduate level, the institute offers M.Tech programmes in VLSI Design, Embedded Systems, Electric Vehicle Technology, Electrical Power Systems, Engineering Design, Computer Science and Engineering, Data Science, and Chemical Engineering.

The vision of establishing BVRIT was to provide high-quality technical education in an environment that fosters the holistic development of students. Over the years, the institute has strengthened its academic reputation through innovative teaching–learning practices, outcome-based education, modern infrastructure, industry-aligned curriculum, and strong placement support. It continues to nurture engineering graduates with strong technical competence, ethical values, and professional skills required for global challenges.

Department of Computer Science & Engineering

The department offers multiple UG and PG programmes with modern laboratories, research groups and strong industry collaborations.

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BVRIT was established in 1997. At present, the department offers five undergraduate programmes: Computer Science and Engineering with an intake of 540 students, CSE–Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with an intake of 180 students, CSE–Data Science with an intake of 180 students, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science with an intake of 180 students, and Computer Science and Business Systems with an intake of 60 students. The department also offers two M.Tech programmes in Computer Science and Engineering and Data Science with an intake of 12 students each.

The department is equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure, advanced computing facilities, high-speed internet, and wireless networks to support quality education and research. The faculty members, with strong teaching and research expertise, are committed to delivering high-quality technical education and fostering innovation among students.

The department actively promotes industry–institute collaboration by identifying emerging areas of interest and engaging in funded research projects, consultancy services, and collaborative initiatives. The major research areas of the department include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Big Data, Internet of Things, Cyber Security, Image Processing, Information Security, Virtual Reality, Blockchain Technology, Parallel Processing, and Human–Computer Interaction.

About the Conference

ICASIS 2027 brings together researchers, academicians, industry experts and innovators to explore Artificial Intelligence for Innovation and Sustainability. The programme features keynote talks, workshops, and panel discussions that highlight practical applications and research breakthroughs. Join us to connect with peers, share ideas, and explore collaborative opportunities.

The International Conference on AI-powered Systems for Innovation and Sustainability (ICASIS 2027) aims to explore the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in driving innovative solutions that promote sustainable development across industries. As the world faces pressing global challenges such as climate change, resource depletion, and rapid urbanization, this conference brings together leading researchers, industry experts, policymakers, and innovators to discuss how AI technologies can accelerate sustainability efforts while fostering innovation.

The conference will focus on cutting-edge advancements in AI and machine learning, particularly in the areas of smart cities, energy optimization, climate prediction, sustainable agriculture, and resource management. It will provide a dynamic platform for sharing research findings, successful applications, and case studies that demonstrate how AI-powered systems can help solve complex environmental and societal problems.

Key topics include AI-driven automation for clean energy, AI for environmental monitoring and climate change mitigation, sustainable manufacturing through AI, and AI-enabled solutions for smart transportation and urban planning. The conference will feature keynote speakers, technical sessions, panel discussions, and workshops that will offer valuable insights into the practical implementation of AI technologies in sustainable practices.

By fostering collaboration across sectors, the conference aims to inspire novel ideas, promote cross-disciplinary research, and identify actionable solutions for building a more sustainable future. Whether you are a researcher, a business leader, or a policymaker, this conference promises to be an intellectually stimulating and professionally rewarding experience, advancing the synergy between AI, innovation, and sustainability.
